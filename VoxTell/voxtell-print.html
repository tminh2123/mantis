VoxTell · CVPR 2026

Free-Text Promptable Universal 3D Medical Image Segmentation

Problem
01 Existing segmentation models are task-specific — one model per organ/pathology, requiring retraining for new targets.
02 Traditional interaction (bounding box, point clicks) requires expert annotation and cannot leverage rich clinical language.
03 3D volumetric segmentation needs to handle arbitrary anatomy described in free-form clinical text ("liver", "aortic arch", "brain tumor").
Proposed — VoxTell
📝
Free-text prompting
Any text from single words to full clinical sentences. Qwen3-4B (frozen) encodes prompts via last-token pooling → 2560-dim embedding.
🏗️
ResEnc-L + DETR Transformer
3D residual encoder (6 stages, 192³ patches). DETR-style decoder generates mask embeddings at 5 scales via text-guided cross-attention.
🌍
Universal — trained on 50+ datasets
Segments arbitrary anatomy in CT/MRI/PET. Fine-tunable on custom datasets with 1-line CLI. First place on ReXGroundingCT benchmark.
