IRIS — Architecture

Dice 84.61% · Text embedding l participates in fusion at 3 scales (14×14, 28×28, 56×56) — same embedding, no scale adaptation. BiFMD handles dim projection internally. Deep supervision on f1, f2 during training.

BiFMD — Internal Structure

Bidirectional FiLM + Cross-Attention + Gated Fusion. Key insight: text modulates image AND image modulates text before attention. Two attention directions merged via learnable gate.

UpBlock — Internal Structure

Skip feature gets PixLevelModule spatial attention before concat. Deep feature is upsampled ×2 via ConvTranspose2d. Output fused through ConvBNReLU layers.
